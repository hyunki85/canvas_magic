Canvas Magic Tutorial

How the trick works

  1. Ask the audience to think of a number from 1 to N.
  2. Choose a color, then casually pretend to draw a few lines on the canvas.
  3. Press and hold the color button to start trick mode.
  4. When the audience says their number, double-tap the matching number cell.
  5. The app automatically plays the drawing for that number.

Tips

  • Before pressing and holding, distract the audience with a natural gesture such as scrolling or changing shapes.
  • You can double-tap anywhere, but tapping near the center helps prevent mistakes.
  • Increase the screen brightness in dark rooms for better visibility.
Tap “Restart Tutorial” on the menu to reset the guide.
